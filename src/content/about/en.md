Hi, I'm **Kyujin** — I make things with data.

I learned to work with data in the field, not from textbooks. Over four
years building bibliographic and archival databases for national
institutions, I wrote the tools that collected, cleaned and inspected
millions of messy, unstructured records. The instinct that formed there —
*data is always dirtier than you think, and good tooling is half the
job* — is what I carry now, studying data science in the UK and building
services in this studio.

## Profile

**University of Sheffield** — MSc Data Science · 2025 ~ (in progress)
Writing a [dissertation](/en/works/rail-delay-dissertation) on delay
patterns and service reliability in UK rail.

**Tech University of Korea** — BSc Computer and Electronics Engineering · 2020 ~ 2024

**MetaSysnet (Seoul)** — Manager, Database Division · Jan 2020 ~ Feb 2024
Built pipelines and field tools for national data construction projects.

- **DAMP** — management program for an art-records digitisation and
  preservation project: data refinement, distribution and inspection
  flows with server-based real-time oversight and admin statistics (C#)
- **KolisMods** — National Authority File project for the National
  Library of Korea: collects MODS bibliographic data and extracts and
  converts just the fields users need (Python)
- **LMM** (Library Metadata Manager) — document database project for the
  National Assembly Library: metadata search, automatic accuracy
  verification, CSV/Excel import and export (Python·Qt)

Alongside: organising large-scale unstructured data, business planning
and running a team.

**Activities** — member of AIS Lab, an inter-university club turning
generative AI into ideas that serve society, and participated in the
Korea Society of Computer and Information winter conference
(Nov 2023 ~ Jan 2024).
